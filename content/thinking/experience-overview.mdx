# Experience Overview

A new leader has just said yes to running the Practicing the Way Course with their group in about three weeks. Unfamiliar territory can leave them feeling the weight of it or possibly overwhelmed and uncertain. So the whole onboarding is crafted as one thing: a counterweight to overwhelm and uncertainty.

> One next step, never many. Confidence leads before connecting.

Two principles carry everything. First, reduce every moment to the single next right thing. Second, steady the leader before opening the circle (inviting group members). Feeling responsible for other people before you feel steady yourself can produce overwhelm instead of confidence.

The disposition I would aim to inspire is calm, prepared, surrendered, confident. The quiet posture of *"I do not need to have the answer. I am a facilitator."*

## What happens first

The opening is deliberately small. A genuine, unhurried beat of encouragement, never hype. A simple sense of what the next few weeks will hold, so the leader never wonders where this is going.

Then exactly one finishable thing: watch session one of the leader training. One completable step can produce outsized steadiness.

## A gentle three-week arc

The shape maps to what a leader needs, in order.

- **Week one, structure and your role.** Steady footing through the first few leader training videos, then the language to invite an existing group well. Not "hit send," but how to start those conversations.
- **Week two, disposition.** Listening. *"I am a facilitator, not the answer."* This is where quiet confidence is built.
- **Week three, readiness.** Prepare for session one specifically. Calm, concrete, *"you are ready."*

The training never blasts out all at once, or in quick succession. If a session is watched, gently nudge the next, and repeat. The leader who is moving is not pushed. The leader who stalls also gets a gentle nudge, with caution around producing guilt.

## The two layers

The experience runs on two layers working in tandem.

The **deterministic layer** is the predetermined heartbeat: trigger-driven email and in-app delivery that surfaces the immediate next step and carries the confidence-building sequence. The in-app surface only ever does one thing at a time: invite the immediate next step, or capture a single data point about how confident the leader feels.

The **[conversation](/conversation)** is the non-deterministic layer. There is enough data to *guess* the next right step, but it is still a guess. The conversation gets closer to the real need. It is an agentic tool with a bounded, legible scope, named by the action a leader is taking, never personified, never called "an AI." At the edge of its purview it points outward to people, a pastor or coordinator, rather than inward to itself. The conversation also functions as an implicit qualitative research tool, a way to understand what leaders actually need.

> Email decides when. In-app is where. The conversation figures out what.
